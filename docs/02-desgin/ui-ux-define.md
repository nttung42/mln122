# UI/UX DESIGN BRIEF — THE VALUE SHIFT

## Product Identity
**Product:** THE VALUE SHIFT  
**Tagline:** Follow the value. Understand the shift. Shape Vietnam's future.

**Product Type:** Immersive Digital Learning Experience.

## 1. UX Direction

Build an exploratory, story-driven and gamified learning experience.

The website should guide users through a journey of discovery rather than presenting information like a conventional educational website or slide deck.

Core UX principles:
- Discovery-first: Present real-world phenomena and questions before introducing theoretical explanations.
- Progressive Disclosure: Simplify complex economic concepts into accessible layers, with optional deeper academic content.
- Story-driven: Connect every concept through a coherent narrative.
- Interactive Learning: Let users explore maps, visualizations, data and economic relationships.
- Meaningful Gamification: Use strategic decisions and simulated outcomes to reinforce learning.

## 2. UI Direction

**Visual Style: Cinematic Editorial + Interactive Data Visualization.**

Combine the visual language of:
- Premium editorial and digital journalism websites.
- Cinematic documentary storytelling.
- Modern data visualization platforms.
- Interactive strategy and simulation games.

The interface should feel premium, immersive, modern, intelligent and approachable.

Avoid:
- Traditional academic website layouts.
- PowerPoint-like slide interfaces.
- Generic corporate dashboard aesthetics.
- Excessive gradients, glassmorphism and decorative animations.
- Dense paragraphs and overly technical terminology.

## 3. Visual Identity

**Color palette:**
- Midnight Navy: #0B1220
- Deep Surface: #151F30
- Electric Blue: #398BCE
- Warm Gold: #E7B85C
- Off White: #F2F4F7
- Muted Gray: #9BA9BA
- Growth Green: #42B88A

**Typography:**
- Headings: Space Grotesk or Sora.
- Body: Inter.
- Numerical data: IBM Plex Mono.

## 4. Interaction & Motion

- Scroll-driven storytelling.
- Smooth section transitions.
- Interactive global maps.
- Animated data visualizations.
- Interactive product journey.
- Expandable explanations and contextual tooltips.
- Responsive decision cards and branching scenarios.
- Animated game results and strategic profiles.

Motion must support comprehension and storytelling, not merely decoration.

## 5. Content Architecture

1. The Paradox — Cinematic opening.
2. Follow the Product — Global product journey.
3. Decode the Value — Simplified economic concepts.
4. The Global Shift — Interactive global value chain map.
5. Vietnam's Position — Evidence-based data storytelling.
6. Vietnam 2040 — Three-round strategic decision game.
7. The Verdict — Personalized strategy report and conclusion.

## 6. Target Audience

University students and general audiences interested in economics, globalization, technology and Vietnam's economic development.

Assume users have limited prior knowledge of Marxist political economy.

Use accessible language, intuitive metaphors, concrete examples and visual explanations without compromising academic accuracy.

## 7. Core Design Goal

Make complex economics feel like an adventure, not a lecture.

Users should leave with:
- A clearer understanding of how global value chains operate.
- An intuitive understanding of surplus value and its relationship to production and ownership.
- A better understanding of Vietnam's position in the global economy.
- The ability to think critically about strategic economic development decisions.
